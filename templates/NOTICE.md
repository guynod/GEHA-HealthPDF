# GEHA form provenance

The current domestic and overseas claim guidance links to the same GEHA Member Claim Submission Form, revision **UMF0022-GEHA 1025 UA**. The bundled blank PDF contains both original pages and retains GEHA's copyright notice.

- Official PDF https://www.mygeha.com/print/UMF0022-GEHA.pdf
- Domestic guidance https://www.geha.com/membership/claims
- Overseas guidance https://www.geha.com/resource-center/medical-member-resources/outside-the-united-states
- Verified on November 20, 2026
- PDF SHA-256 `a74a4f0decd3dd2bd82d705e40e25ac6088064d3245b8365c3357eb631d22497`

The official endpoint timed out from the build machine. The PDF was retrieved through Google's public document viewer using the official URL, then verified for both pages, the printed revision, filing instructions and form fields against the live official source. No personal profile or completed claim was used to create this template.

The template mapping is bound to those exact PDF bytes. After a publisher update, obtain the new blank form, inspect its fields and visually verify newly generated domestic and overseas examples before updating the mapping. Existing submitted claims keep their own prior template and evidence history.
